Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

About this application

SSS Trading Reports generates daily, weekly, and monthly PDF reports for its operator.

Google account access

The application requests the Google Gmail send permission to send trading reports by email. It does not request permission to read the user's Gmail inbox.

Data use and sharing

Trading information is used to generate the configured reports. Report content is sent to the email recipient configured by the operator. Google account authorization credentials are used to authenticate email sending and should be stored securely.

The application does not sell personal information. Report contents and operational logs may be stored by the operator as part of the trading reporting service.

Security and contact

The operator should restrict access to reports and authorization credentials. For privacy questions, contact sss.bot.agent@gmail.com.
